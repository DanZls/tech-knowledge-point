## How do you handle pressure or tight production deadlines?

"I handle pressure by **prioritizing tasks systematically** and maintaining **transparent communication**. When a deadline is tight, I break down the remaining deliverables into a granular checklist. If I foresee a bottleneck—such as a complex third-party integration or an optimization issue—I flag it to my manager or lead early with proposed solutions, rather than waiting until the final deadline day. This ensures the team can realign or descope non-critical items if necessary."

## Describe a situation where you had a conflict with a teammate or manager and how you resolved it.

"In a recent project, we had a disagreement regarding how to handle an attendance summary report column (`AgendaName`). The system was recording conflicting entries like 'Market Visit' and 'End Day' simultaneously. The team lead initially preferred a quick system log fix, while I felt a **defined priority hierarchy** based on actual business activity was necessary to avoid data corruption in reports.
Instead of arguing, I pulled sample database records, mapped out how the conflicting data looked downstream in reporting, and presented a clear priority logic. By showing the **technical impact objectively**, my lead agreed with my approach, and we implemented the hierarchy smoothly."

## Adapting your communication style across Agile roles.

"In an Agile team, I've learned that communication needs to be tailored to the audience. With **fellow developers**, I keep things technical and concise—focusing on implementation details, edge cases, and code-level tradeoffs during stand-ups or pull request reviews. With **QA**, I make sure to be explicit about expected behavior, boundary conditions, and any schema or API contract changes so testing stays aligned with the actual implementation.
When talking to **Product Owners or Business Analysts**, I shift away from technical jargon and instead frame things in terms of **business impact, timelines, and tradeoffs**—for example, explaining that a change will improve report generation speed rather than discussing the specific optimization technique used. With **Scrum Masters**, I focus on **blockers, dependencies, and progress against sprint commitments** so they can facilitate the team effectively. Overall, I adjust the level of technical depth and vocabulary based on who I'm speaking with, while keeping the core message clear and actionable."
